## Getting Started

1. **Create Your Local Vault:** On first launch, Mepp creates a hardware-backed SQLCipher AES-256 database on your device and displays your **30-character offline Recovery Key**. Store this Recovery Key safely (e.g., in your password manager) — PEBCAK Consulting LLC has zero servers and cannot recover lost keys.
2. **Explore Starter Packs or Sample Sandbox:** Pick curated habit starter packs or preview Mepp with the isolated Sample Data Sandbox before committing your own routines.
3. **Plan Today & Track Growth:** Complete daily quests and rituals on **Today**, track 15-minute blocks on **Log**, and review Year-in-Pixels mood maps, Annual/Quarterly Goals, Custom Metrics, Decisions, and Guided Weekly Reviews on **Growth**.

## Encrypted Backups (`.meppbackup`) & Device Migration

- **Exporting a Backup:** Open **Settings → Backups & Export → Export Encrypted `.meppbackup`**. Choose any destination folder or cloud provider via Android's Storage Access Framework or iOS Files.
- **Restoring on a New Phone (Cross-Platform iOS ↔ Android):** Open Mepp on your new Android or iOS device, select **Restore from `.meppbackup`**, choose your archive file, and enter your 30-character Recovery Key. Both platforms share the identical `MEPPBK01` AES-256-GCM + SQLCipher v20 format.

## Wear OS & Apple Watch Companion

- **Wear OS 4/5:** Install Mepp on both your Android phone and paired Wear OS watch. Swipe across the 3-page rotary watch app (Progress Ring & Streak, Today's Quests, Quick Mood & Focus) and add the Mepp Progress Tile or Watch Face Complication.
- **watchOS:** Install the Mepp companion app via the Apple Watch app on your paired iPhone for wrist progress rings, quest check-offs, and focus status.

## Restoring In-App Purchases

If you purchased **Mepp Ad-Free Lifetime** (`com.pebcakconsulting.mepp.pro.lifetime`) and switched devices or reinstalled:
1. Ensure your device is signed into the same **Google Play** or **Apple ID** account used for purchase.
2. Open **Settings → Support Mepp / Ad-Free Pro → Restore Purchases**.
3. Your lifetime ad-free entitlement restores immediately at no charge.

---

## Deleting Your Data

Mepp stores your vault exclusively on your device:
- Open **Settings → Security & Data → Wipe / Reset Local Vault** (or uninstall Mepp / clear app storage in OS settings) to permanently erase all local records.
