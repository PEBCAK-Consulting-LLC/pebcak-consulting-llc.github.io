# Radical Rush

### 90s Cartoon 24-Vehicle Arcade Stunt Runner for Phones & Tablets

**Radical Rush** (`com.pebcakconsulting.radicalrush`) is a kid-safe, vertical one-thumb vehicle stunt runner built for iOS, iPadOS, and Android at up to **120Hz**. Carve across 5 wide neon lanes, launch off glowing ramps for aerial Stunt Combos, spark Elevated Neon Grind Rails, and pilot **24 original cartoon vehicles**!

---

## Key Features

### 🚀 24 Original Cartoon Vehicles Across 6 Classes
- **4 Unlocked Starter Rides:** Jump straight in with *Sky Viper F-94* (Jet), *Mega Crusher 4x4* (Monster Rig), *Star Viper X-99* (Spaceship), or *Neon Blade 900* (Motorcycle).
- **20 Earnable Unlocks & 4 Paint Wraps Per Ride:** Unlock cartoon Planes, Bikes, Monster Rigs, Starships/UFOs, Hover-Decks, and Arcade Karts strictly through gameplay Coins and Career Points on the Trophy Road—plus test-drive locked rides mid-run through **Holo-Morph Portals**!

### 🌴 6 Dynamic 90s Biomes, Grind Rails & Sky Bonus Stage
- Race from gentle beginner speeds into high-tempo runs across *Sunset Boardwalk*, *Laser Mall Atrium*, *Volcano Dino Canyon*, *Glacier Half-Pipe*, *Cyber Grid Highway*, and *Starlight Orbit*.
- Spark **Elevated Neon Grind Rails**, warp into the hazard-free 7-second **Sky-Warp Cloud Bonus Tunnel**, and spell **R-A-D-I-C-A-L** to crack open +500 Coin Mystery Crates!

### 🤖 Robo-Buddy Sidekicks, Toy Blasters & Cartoon Bosses
- Fly alongside 4 Robo-Buddy Sidekicks and upgrade kid-friendly toy blasters (*Foam Darts*, *90s Green Slime*, *Mega Rainbow Bubbles*, and *Laser-Tag Prisms*) up to Level 10.
- Outsmart Saturday-morning cartoon bosses including *Dr. Rewind*, *Baroness Boombox*, and *Lord Dial-Up*!

### 🔒 100% Kid-Safe & Family-Friendly by Design
- **Zero Personal Data Collection:** No accounts, no logins, no chat, no loot boxes, and zero behavioral tracking (`AD_ID` removed on Android; no ATT/IDFA tracking on iOS).
- **Free Ad-Free Passes & Hardened Parental Gate:** Earn free 1-Hour & 6-Hour Ad-Free Passes in-app, with all parent settings and optional one-time **Radical Club Pro** (`com.pebcakconsulting.radicalrush.pro.lifetime`) lifetime ad-free unlock protected behind a randomized adult arithmetic Parental Gate.
- **Cross-Device Cloud Save Sync:** Sync unlocked vehicles, wraps, coins, Holo-Pogs, leaderboards, and achievements via **Apple iCloud & Game Center** (iOS/iPadOS) or **Google Play Games Services & Android Cloud Backup** (Android).
