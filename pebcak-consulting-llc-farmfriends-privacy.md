# Farm Friends: Overworld Rescue — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026  
**Application:** Farm Friends: Overworld Rescue (iOS, iPadOS, Android)  
**Publisher:** PEBCAK Consulting LLC  
**Developer Contact:** [ryan@pebcakconsulting.com](mailto:ryan@pebcakconsulting.com) | [support@pebcakconsulting.com](mailto:support@pebcakconsulting.com)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Children's Privacy Commitment

PEBCAK Consulting LLC is deeply committed to protecting the privacy of children and families. **Farm Friends: Overworld Rescue** is designed under strict compliance with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K)**, and **Apple App Store Review Guidelines 1.3 and 5.1.4 (Kids Category)**.

- **Zero Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, request, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) from children or adults.
- **No Account Creation:** Players do not need an account, username, password, email address, or phone number to play the game.
- **Zero Third-Party Tracking / Zero Behavioral Profiling:** We do not track children across third-party websites or apps. The Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is completely disabled.
- **Local-First Sandboxed Persistence:** All game progress, rescued animal companions, unlocked stickers, and bank coins are saved strictly on the local device.

---

## 2. Information We Process and Store

### A. Local Game Progression (On-Device Only)
- **Data Stored:** Rescued animal inventory, daily quest progress, sticker book completion, character appearance customization choices, high scores, and audio preferences.
- **Storage Location:** Sandboxed within the operating system's local application data folder.
- **Cloud Backup:** Included exclusively in your device's native **Apple iCloud Backup** or **Google Device Backup**. PEBCAK Consulting LLC does not operate external servers or hold copies of your game saves.

### B. Family-Safe Advertising (COPPA Compliant)
- **Partner:** Google AdMob (Families Program).
- **COPPA Child-Directed Configuration:** All ad requests are flagged with `tagForChildDirectedTreatment=true` and `underAgeOfConsent=true` with a maximum content rating of **G** (General Audience).
- **Non-Personalized Ads Only:** Advertisements are strictly contextual and family-safe. Behavioral targeting, remarketing, and interest-based profiling are prohibited.
- **Parental Control Ad Toggle:** Inside the gated Parent Zone, parents have the option to completely turn OFF optional rewarded ads. When toggled off, no ad requests are made.

### C. In-App Purchases (Protected by Parental Gate)
- **Platform Billing:** Processed directly by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing.
- **Parental Gate Requirement:** In compliance with Apple Guideline 1.3, every purchase attempt is shielded behind an adult math verification challenge to prevent accidental child purchases.
- **Purchase Data:** PEBCAK Consulting LLC never receives or stores payment credentials, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information through a support email inquiry, please contact us immediately at [ryan@pebcakconsulting.com](mailto:ryan@pebcakconsulting.com), and we will promptly delete such correspondence.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Links

Farm Friends: Overworld Rescue contains zero external links accessible to children. All support and policy links are located exclusively within the **Parent Zone** behind our adult math challenge.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Because all game data is stored exclusively on your local device, you maintain complete ownership and control:
- **Erase All Data:** You can reset or erase your game progress anytime by deleting the application from your device or using the in-game reset option located in the Parent Zone.
