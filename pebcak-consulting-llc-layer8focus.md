# Layer8Focus

### Tactical Zero-Knowledge Focus Timer & System Telemetry for Engineers & Knowledge Workers

In computer networking, the OSI reference model defines 7 structural layers — from physical copper to application protocols. In IT folklore, **Layer 8** is the human operator: notoriously the most critical yet error-prone link in the system.

**Layer8Focus** is a high-performance productivity HUD engineered specifically for developers, system administrators, DevOps operators, cybersecurity analysts, and deep-work professionals. Combining an authentic retro CRT terminal aesthetic with modern zero-drift telemetry, Layer8Focus eliminates distraction and keeps human operators in peak flow state.

## Key Capabilities

### ⏱️ Monotonic Zero-Drift Timer Engine
Traditional interval timers lose precision when apps enter background mode or devices sleep. Layer8Focus uses continuous monotonic hardware clock differential calculations, ensuring your focus cycles never drift a single millisecond.

### 🔒 100% Local-First AES-256-GCM Encryption
Your productivity habits and session logs are nobody's business but your own. All interval durations, daily streaks, custom presets, and session notes are encrypted locally at rest using hardware-accelerated **AES-256-GCM**. No remote analytics, no cross-site trackers, and no mandatory cloud accounts.

### 💾 Password-Protected Cloud Vaults (.l8bak)
Take complete sovereignty over your data. Export your encrypted database into a password-protected `.l8bak` binary archive using **PBKDF2** (100,000 hashing rounds). Store it on local storage, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive, and restore it seamlessly onto any new device.

### ⌚ Apple Watch Companion App
Never look away from your terminal or workstation. The companion app for Apple Watch (watchOS 10+) mirrors active session countdowns in real time with distinct wrist haptic alerts when focus and break intervals conclude.

### 📱 Live Activities & Dynamic Island
Keep your active countdown visible at a glance directly from your iPhone Lock Screen, StandBy display, and Dynamic Island while multitasking across other tools.

### 🎧 Procedural Soundscapes & Binaural Entrainment
Immerse yourself in curated audio environments:
- **Server Room Hum** (Cooling fans and rack harmonics)
- **Mechanical Keyboard** (Tactile switch click-clack rhythms)
- **Terminal Noise** (Phosphor CRT hum and brown noise frequencies)
- **Binaural Entrainment** (Targeted brainwave stimulation frequencies)

### 🌿 Dual Aesthetics: Terminal & Zen Minimalist
Toggle between a classic **VT100 Phosphor Green / Amber CRT Terminal** HUD and a serene, distraction-free **Zen Minimalist** layout depending on your work environment.

### 🧘 Meditation Sanctuary & Somatic Breathwork
Guided mindfulness sessions, customizable breathwork cadences, and a 3-stem procedural DSP audio mixer to recharge between high-intensity focus blocks.
