## How to Play & Solve Mysteries

1. **Explore Mini-Boston:** Tap anywhere on the street—or use the on-screen joystick—to walk around 6 food-themed Boston districts.
2. **Enter the Crime Scene:** Tap the glowing **CRIME SCENE** marker (or open **HQ** to choose any of the 26 handcrafted mysteries).
3. **Uncover 3 Clues & Solve the Lab/Puzzle:** Tap numbered evidence spots (`#1`–`#8`) to uncover all **3 Clues**, dust for prints or scan with UV in **FORENSICS**, and solve the case's **PUZZLE** riddle or cipher.
4. **Pin the Corkboard & Issue the Warrant:** Open **BOARD**, match **WHO + WHERE + PROOF**, and tap **VERIFY WARRANT** before accusing the culprit!
5. **In-Game Field Guide:** Tap **`? GUIDE`** in the top menu bar anytime to search or browse all 8 chapters of the Detective Field Guide.

## Large-Print Clue Reader & Boston Dialect Toggle

- Every discovered clue, case briefing, suspect alibi, and forensic scan opens in a spacious **28pt Closeable Clue Popup** that stays on screen until you tap **`GOT IT! CLOSE ✓`** or **`X`**.
- Tap **`TEXT: BOS` / `TEXT: ENG`** inside the popup header (or **`CC`** in Precinct HQ) anytime to switch between playful Boston slang and plain Standard English.
- Tap **`CLUES: X/3`** in the Crime Scene top bar to review the Case Briefing and all discovered clues in one Dossier view.

## Kiwi Character Designer & Flying Kiwi Bird

- Tap **`FIT`** in the top HUD (or **`REDESIGN CHARACTER`** in Precinct HQ) to customize your Detective Callsign, coat, hat, glasses, shoes, Kiwi flesh tint, and food pet sidekick.
- Tap **`FLY`** in the bottom-right corner of the city map to transform from a Kiwi Fruit into a flapping Kiwi Bird and soar across Boston!

## Parental Controls & Parent Zone

- Open **HQ** and tap **PARENT ZONE** (or access via Settings) and complete the 3-step adult math verification gate + 2-second hold confirmation.
- **Rewarded Ad Kill-Switch:** Parents can disable optional bonus video ads with one tap so children play 100% offline/naturally.
- **In-App Privacy Policy Viewer:** View the full COPPA/GDPR-K privacy summary directly inside the Parent Zone without leaving the app.

## In-App Purchases & Restoring Purchases

Wicked P.I. offers optional parent-gated passes to remove ad banners:
- **24-Hour Ad-Free Pass (\$0.99)** (`com.pebcakconsulting.wickedpi.adfree24h`)
- **Lifetime Ad-Free Unlock (\$4.99)** (`com.pebcakconsulting.wickedpi.adfreelifetime`)

**How to Restore Purchases on a New Device:**
1. Open **HQ -> Parent Zone** and complete the adult math challenge.
2. Tap **RESTORE PURCHASES**.
3. Apple StoreKit will immediately restore your Lifetime Ad-Free Unlock at zero cost.

## Cloud Backup & Game Center

- Game saves are stored locally on-device and backed up privately to your personal **Apple iCloud Key-Value Store** (`SAVE TO iCLOUD` / `LOAD iCLOUD` in Precinct HQ).
- Detective Points, Stars, and Badges sync automatically with **Apple Game Center** when signed in on iOS/iPadOS.
