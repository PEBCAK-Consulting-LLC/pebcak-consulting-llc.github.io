## Radical Rush (`com.pebcakconsulting.radicalrush`) — Summary for Parents & Reviewers

- **Zero Custom Servers & Zero PII:** *Radical Rush* collects zero names, emails, phone numbers, photos, contacts, microphone/camera input, or precise location, and requires no accounts or logins.
- **Zero Advertising ID (`AD_ID` / IDFA):** `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` and AdServices permissions are removed on Android, and Apple ATT/IDFA is never requested on iOS (`NSPrivacyTracking = false`).
- **Child-Directed, G-Rated Contextual Ads Only:** Google AdMob is locked *before* initialization (`GADDelayAppMeasurementInit` / `DELAY_APP_MEASUREMENT_INIT`) to `tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true`, `tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true`, `maxAdContentRating = "G"`, and `nonPersonalizedAds = true`.
- **Hardened Adult Parental Gate:** All parent settings, external links, and the optional one-time **Radical Club Pro** (`com.pebcakconsulting.radicalrush.pro.lifetime`) ad-free unlock are protected behind a randomized multi-digit adult arithmetic Parental Gate.
