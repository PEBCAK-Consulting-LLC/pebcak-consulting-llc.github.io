# PEBCAK Puzzles

### 1,200+ Offline Travel Activity Book Puzzles, Word Searches, Logic Grids, Mysteries & Mazes

**PEBCAK Puzzles** (`com.pebcakconsulting.pebcakpuzzles`) is an airplane-ready, zero-tracking digital travel activity book built for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Flip through **30 original variety puzzle modes** and **1,200 pre-verified offline pages** across **Easy**, **Difficult**, and **Frustrating** tiers—with universal 2-finger pinch-to-zoom and a dedicated Visually Accessible + Global Black & White mode.

---

## Key Features

### 📖 30 Variety Puzzle Modes & 1,200 Offline Omnibus Pages
- **Chapter A — Search & Spot:** Themed Word Search, Hidden-Path Trail, Twin-Scene Spotter, Symbol Count, Pattern Odd-One-Out, and Coordinate Scavenger.
- **Chapter B — Word & Cipher:** Travel Crossword, Cryptic Quote Cipher, Word Ladder, Anagram Scrambler, Missing-Vowel Phrases, and Category Word-Link.
- **Chapter C — Number & Logic Grids:** 9x9 Number Grid, Cage-Sum Grid, Star-Region Placement, Island-Bridge Network, Row/Column Skyscraper Heights, and Mosaic Clue Painter.
- **Chapter D — Mysteries & Riddles:** Whodunit Elimination Grid, Alibi Timeline Sorter, Lateral-Thinking Riddles, Safe-Cracker Codebreaker, Seating-Chart Deduction, and Map Route Planner.
- **Chapter E — Mazes & Backseat Games:** Classic Winding Maze, Ice-Slide Labyrinth, Dots & Boxes Duel, Four-in-a-Row Strategy, License-Plate Word Builder, and Memory Flip Pairs.

### 🔍 Built for Real Travel & Visual Comfort
- **3 Upfront Difficulty Tiers:** Switch anytime between **Easy** (relaxed warm-up), **Difficult** (classic newspaper crunch), and **Frustrating** (deep brain-burner).
- **Visual Mode Onboarding & Global Black & White:** Choose **Dark Mode**, **Light Mode**, or **Visually Accessible Mode** (21:1 high-contrast typography & thick borders), plus a **Global Rec.709 Black & White** e-ink filter.
- **Universal 2-Finger Pinch-to-Zoom:** Pinch any clue text to reflow up to `2.5x`, or pinch any puzzle grid with an automatic precision magnifier loupe.

### 🛂 Travel Passport, Flight-Hours Odometer & Trophy Road
- Earn **36 Travel Passport Stamps**, **60 Merit Badges**, **50 Traveler Ranks**, **Flight-Hours Chests**, **Daily Travel Bounties**, and **Weekly 7-Stop Expeditions**—all playable 100% offline with zero timers or loot boxes.

### 🔒 100% Family-Safe & Zero Behavioral Tracking
- **Zero Personal Data Collection:** No accounts, no logins, no chat, and zero behavioral tracking (`AD_ID` removed on Android; zero ATT/IDFA tracking on iOS).
- **Free Ad-Free Flight Passes & Hardened Parental Gate:** Claim free 1-Hour & 6-Hour Ad-Free Flight Passes (including an offline Airplane-Mode pass), or unlock **PEBCAK Puzzles Pro** (`com.pebcakconsulting.pebcakpuzzles.pro.lifetime`) forever behind an adult arithmetic Parental Gate.
