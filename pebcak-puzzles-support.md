## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Does PEBCAK Puzzles work 100% offline in Airplane Mode?
Yes! All **30 puzzle modes** and all **1,200 pre-verified Omnibus catalog puzzles** are stored locally on your device. Even the **1-Hour and 6-Hour Ad-Free Flight Passes** include an offline Airplane-Mode spotlight fallback when you have no Wi-Fi or cellular signal.

### 2. How do I change Visual Modes, Black & White Mode, or Text Size?
- Tap the **Gear (Settings)** button in the top-right corner of the Activity Book Hub or any puzzle screen to switch between **Dark Mode**, **Light Mode**, and **Visually Accessible Mode** (`21:1` contrast), or toggle **Global Black & White Mode**.
- Use a **2-finger pinch** anywhere on clue text to scale typography up to `2.5x`, or pinch any puzzle board to zoom up to `3.0x` with the precision magnifier loupe.

### 3. How do I mute Background Music or Sound Effects while listening to podcasts?
Tap the **Gear (Settings)** button and toggle **Background Music** and **Sound Effects** independently. Audio is configured in ambient mix-with-others mode so your podcasts and audiobooks keep playing uninterrupted.

### 4. How do I restore my PEBCAK Puzzles Pro purchase on a new device?
1. Tap the **Ad-Free Flight Pass** banner at the bottom of the Hub (or open **Settings**).
2. Tap **Parents: Restore Purchases** and solve the adult multiplication Parental Gate.
3. Ensure you are signed into the same Apple ID or Google Play account used for the original `com.pebcakconsulting.pebcakpuzzles.pro.lifetime` purchase.
