## Quick Controls & How to Play

- **Swipe Left / Right:** Carve across the 5 neon arcade lanes (swiping mid-air performs **Barrel Rolls & Corkscrews** for bonus stunt combos!).
- **Swipe Up:** Jump over low barriers, hop onto **Elevated Neon Grind Rails**, or chain aerial flips.
- **Swipe Down:** Power-slide under overhead gates on the ground, or trigger a **Hyper Slam** shockwave from mid-air.
- **Quick Tap:** Fire your equipped kid-friendly toy blaster (*Foam Darts*, *90s Green Slime*, *Mega Rainbow Bubbles*, or *Laser-Tag Prisms*) or activate **Radical Overdrive** when charged.

## Restoring Radical Club Pro (`$4.99` Lifetime Ad-Free Unlock)

If you reinstall *Radical Rush* or set up a new phone or tablet:
1. Open **Radical Rush** and tap **PARENTS** (top-right of the Main Menu) or the **AD-FREE** banner.
2. Solve the randomized adult arithmetic **Parental Gate**.
3. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
4. Apple StoreKit 2 (iOS/iPadOS) or Google Play Billing (Android) will verify your account receipt and restore your lifetime ad-free **Radical Club Pro** (`com.pebcakconsulting.radicalrush.pro.lifetime`) entitlement immediately at no charge.

## Cross-Device Cloud Save Sync

- **iOS & iPadOS:** Sign into **iCloud** and **Game Center** in iOS Settings, then open **PARENTS -> Cloud Save Sync** (or **POGS & QUESTS -> TROPHIES**) to sync unlocked vehicles, wraps, coins, leaderboards, and achievements across your iPhone and iPad.
- **Android:** Sign into **Google Play Games**, then open **PARENTS -> Cloud Save Sync** to sync your career progress, high scores, and achievements across Android phones and tablets.
