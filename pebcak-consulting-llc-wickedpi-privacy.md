# Wicked P.I. — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026  
**Application:** Wicked P.I. (`com.pebcakconsulting.wickedpi` — iOS, iPadOS, Android)  
**Publisher:** PEBCAK Consulting LLC  
**Developer Contact:** [ryan@pebcakconsulting.com](mailto:ryan@pebcakconsulting.com) | [support@pebcakconsulting.com](mailto:support@pebcakconsulting.com)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Children's Privacy Commitment

PEBCAK Consulting LLC is deeply committed to protecting the privacy of children, teens, and families. **Wicked P.I.** is designed under strict compliance with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K)**, and **Apple App Store Review Guidelines**.

- **Zero Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, request, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) from children or adults.
- **No Account Creation & Kid-Safe Callsigns Only:** Players never enter free-form names, emails, or passwords. Detective callsigns are chosen strictly from 8 pre-approved preset titles.
- **Zero Third-Party Tracking / Zero Behavioral Profiling:** We do not track players across third-party websites or apps. The Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is never requested or accessed (`NSPrivacyTracking = false`).
- **Local-First Sandboxed Persistence:** All solved mysteries, stars, unlocked outfits, stickers, and coins are saved locally on your device (with optional private backup in your personal Apple iCloud Key-Value store).

---

## 2. Information We Process and Store

### A. Local Game Progression & Personal iCloud Sync
- **Data Stored:** Solved case stars, detective rank, unlocked wardrobe outfits, sticker album completion, coin balance, and audio/caption settings.
- **Storage Location:** Sandboxed within the operating system's local application container and your personal Apple iCloud Key-Value container / Game Center achievements (when signed into Apple's system services). PEBCAK Consulting LLC operates zero external game servers and cannot view or access your save files.

### B. Family-Safe Contextual Advertising (COPPA Compliant)
- **Partner:** Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites){:rel="noopener noreferrer"} and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy){:rel="noopener noreferrer"}.
- **COPPA Child-Directed Configuration:** Every ad request is strictly flagged with `tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true`, `tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true`, `maxAdContentRating = "G"` (General Audience), and `npa = 1` (Non-Personalized Ads Only).
- **Non-Personalized Ads Only:** Advertisements are contextual and family-safe. Behavioral targeting, remarketing, and cross-app tracking are disabled.
- **Parental Control Ad Kill-Switch:** Inside the gated **Parent Zone**, parents can completely disable optional rewarded bonus videos at any time, or unlock ad-free play via optional passes.

### C. In-App Purchases (Protected by Hardened Parental Gate)
- **Platform Billing:** Processed exclusively by Apple App Store (**StoreKit**) or Google Play Billing.
- **3-Step Parental Gate:** Every purchase button and restore action is shielded behind a multi-step adult math and word-number verification challenge plus a 2-second hold confirmation to prevent accidental child taps.
- **Purchase Data:** PEBCAK Consulting LLC never receives or stores payment credentials, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions). If you believe a child has sent personal information via an email support inquiry, please contact us at [ryan@pebcakconsulting.com](mailto:ryan@pebcakconsulting.com) and we will promptly delete that correspondence.

## 4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Because game progression is stored locally on your device (and optionally in your personal Apple iCloud container), you retain complete control:
- **Erase Data:** Delete the application from your device or manage your personal iCloud app storage in iOS Settings to remove all saved progress immediately.
