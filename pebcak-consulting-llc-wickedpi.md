# Wicked P.I.

### Kid-Friendly Teen-Noir Food Detective Mystery Adventure in Mini-Boston

**Wicked P.I.** is a family-friendly graphic-novel mystery and puzzle adventure built for iOS, iPadOS, and Android. Step into the trenchcoat of **Detective Kip**—a wisecracking Kiwi Fruit private investigator who transforms into a flapping Kiwi Bird to fly across a 100% food-based Mini-Boston!

---

## Key Capabilities

### 🥝 Design Your Own Kiwi Detective & Callsign
Start your career in the **Kiwi Character Studio**! Choose from 8 kid-safe Detective Callsigns, 8 full-body detective coats, 11 hats, eyewear, props, custom Kiwi flesh colors, and 6 loyal food sidekicks like **Munchkin the Donut Hole** and **Pinchy the Baby Lobstah**.

### 🔎 26 Handcrafted Boston Mysteries & Large-Print Clue Popups
Investigate 6 iconic Mini-Boston food districts—from the **North End Cannoli Safe Heist** and **Harbor Chowdah Wharf** to the **Emerald Broccoli & Pepper Ballpark Wall**! Tap crime scene spots to read clues in an easy-to-read **28pt Closeable Clue Reader Popup** with a live **Boston Slang / Standard English** toggle (`TEXT: BOS / ENG`) and an anytime **Case Clue Dossier**.

### 📌 Red-String Corkboard, Forensics Lab & Sleuth Riddles
Dust for pastry powder prints, sweep scenes with a 365nm UV beam, piece together torn receipt scraps, crack ciphers and logic riddles, and pin **WHO + WHERE + PROOF** on the Red-String Corkboard to issue an official Arrest Warrant!

### 📖 Built-In Searchable "How to Play" Detective Field Guide
Includes an 8-chapter interactive Field Guide with live keyword search, step-by-step navigation, instant skip/exit, and one-tap replay from Precinct HQ or the top HUD (`? GUIDE`).

### 🔒 100% COPPA-Compliant Family-Safe Design
- **Zero Personal Data Collection:** No accounts, no logins, no chat, and zero behavioral tracking.
- **Hardened 3-Step Parent Zone:** All optional purchases, restore actions, privacy info, and the Rewarded Ad Kill-Switch are protected behind a multi-step adult math verification gate.
